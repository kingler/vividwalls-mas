# B2B Outreach & Trade Sales

## Offer (confirm with the owner before the first send)
- Trade discount 20% off retail (floor: 1.8× Pictorem cost, see `business/catalog-and-pricing.md`)
- Volume: 5+ pieces 25% off; custom sizes and series curation for a space
- Net-30 invoicing for approved accounts (Shopify draft orders / B2B invoices)
- Direct-to-site shipping, white-label packaging

## Steps
1. **Prospect**: pick a segment and city (start with NYC metro). Find 10–20 targets using web
   research. Record name, company, role, website, email or contact page, why they fit
   (recent project, style match).
2. **Qualify**: does their portfolio show modern or contemporary interiors with large wall
   spaces? Score A/B/C.
3. **Draft** (Gmail drafts only): ≤120 words, a specific compliment on a recent project,
   2 pieces that would suit their style (with links), the trade offer, and one easy CTA
   ("want a curated set of 5 for your next project?").
4. **Follow up**: day 3 (short nudge with a room mockup), day 8 (last note plus lookbook).
   Stop after 3 touches.
5. **Quote**: when a prospect replies, build a quote (pieces, sizes, trade price, shipping,
   lead time) and a Shopify draft order after approval.
6. **Pipeline**: update `ops/pipeline.md`:
   `| Company | Segment | Contact | Stage (prospect/contacted/replied/quoted/won/lost) | Next step | Date |`

## Guardrails
Never send email, create invoices, or promise pricing below the floor without explicit
owner approval. Respect opt-outs. Comply with CAN-SPAM (include sender identity and an
opt-out line).
