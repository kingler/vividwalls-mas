# VividWalls — Company Profile

## What we sell
Original contemporary abstract & geometric art, printed on demand as museum-quality canvas.
Each piece belongs to a named series; the series are the backbone of merchandising.

| Series (Shopify collection) | Pieces | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Fractal | 6 | Symmetrical geometric explosions — reds, noir, dark |
| Mosaic | 6 | Tiled color fields — vivid and noir variants |
| Intersecting Perspectives | 5 | Overlapping planes and angles |
| Parallelogram Illusion | 4 | Optical geometric layering |
| Kimono | 4 | Symmetric, textile-inspired forms |

37 artworks are live (Oct 2026). Formats: gallery-wrapped stretched canvas or canvas roll,
in 24x36, 36x48 and 53x72 inches.

## Positioning
- **Statement art for modern interiors** — large-format, bold, ready to hang.
- Gallery quality at a fraction of original-art prices ($153–$551).
- Brand voice: confident, design-literate, warm; exclusive but never snobbish. Talk about
  how a piece changes a room, not about "digital techniques".

## Who buys (target segments)
1. **Design-forward homeowners & renters, 30–60, US** — furnishing a new home or living
   room refresh; discover on Pinterest, Instagram, Google Images.
2. **Interior designers & home stagers (B2B)** — need multiple large pieces fast, repeat buyers,
   expect a trade discount.
3. **Hospitality, offices & real-estate developers (B2B)** — lobbies, Airbnb/short-term
   rentals, model units, co-working; buy in bulk, care about lead time and invoicing.
4. **Gift buyers** — housewarmings, holidays; smaller sizes, canvas roll.

## Fulfillment model
Pictorem prints, stretches and ships direct to the customer under our brand.
No inventory risk; the trade-off is lead time (print + ship) and shipping cost on 53x72.
